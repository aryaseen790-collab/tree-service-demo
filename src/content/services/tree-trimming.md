A well-trimmed tree does more than look nice. It drops fewer branches in storms, lets more light reach your lawn and keeps limbs off your roof. In {{city}}, where summers are hot and spring storms can be rough, smart pruning is one of the best things you can do to protect your trees and your home.

## Signs your trees need trimming

You don't need to guess. Call for a trim when you notice:

- **Branches touching or hanging over your roof.** They scrape shingles, drop leaves in gutters and give squirrels a highway onto your house.
- **Dead branches** that have no leaves in summer. Deadwood can fall without warning.
- **Low limbs** that block your driveway, sidewalk, street signs or the view from your windows.
- **A crowded, thick crown** where wind can't pass through. Dense trees catch more wind in a storm.
- **Branches that cross and rub**, or a tree that has grown lopsided.
- **Storm damage** like hanging, cracked or split limbs (we call these "hangers").

## What good pruning looks like

The goal is to help the tree, not just make it smaller. We follow standard pruning practices used by tree care pros:

- **Clean cuts at the right spot.** We cut just outside the branch collar, the slightly swollen area where a branch meets the trunk. That's where the tree heals best.
- **No topping.** Topping and "lion-tailing" (stripping all inner branches) weaken trees. We don't do it.
- **Don't take too much.** Removing too many leaves at once stresses the tree. We remove only what's needed.
- **Right season for oaks.** Oak wilt is a serious threat in Central Texas. We follow seasonal guidance for oaks and paint fresh oak cuts. Learn more on our [oak wilt page](/oak-wilt/).

## Our trimming process

1. **Walk-through.** We look at your trees with you and ask what's bothering you, like roof clearance, shade or messy limbs.
2. **Clear plan and quote.** You'll know which branches we're cutting and why, plus the price, before we start.
3. **Set up safely.** We rope off the work zone and protect plants, patios and fences.
4. **Prune.** Our crew climbs or uses a lift to make each cut, lowering heavy limbs with ropes when needed.
5. **Clean up.** Branches go in the chipper, and we rake and blow off your yard and driveway.

## Types of trimming we do

- **Crown cleaning:** removing dead, dying, broken and diseased branches.
- **Crown thinning:** taking out select branches to let light and wind through.
- **Crown raising:** lifting low limbs for clearance over lawns, driveways and roofs.
- **Crown reduction:** carefully shortening limbs on a tree that's grown too big for its space.
- **Young tree pruning:** shaping young trees early so they grow strong.

## What affects the cost of trimming

Every job is different, so we quote after seeing the trees. Price depends on:

- **Tree size and number of trees.** Bigger trees and more trees take more time.
- **How much needs to come out.** A light clean-up is quicker than heavy storm repair.
- **Access.** Trees in a fenced backyard or over a house may need climbing instead of a lift.
- **Nearby hazards** like roofs, pools or power lines that call for extra care.
- **Cleanup and hauling.** Removing all debris is part of our quote, so you know the full picture.
