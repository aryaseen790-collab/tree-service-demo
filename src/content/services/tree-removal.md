Taking down a tree is a big decision. A good tree adds shade, privacy and value to your home. But a tree that's dead, cracked or leaning toward your house is a real risk, especially in a part of Texas where spring storms bring strong winds and hail. We help homeowners in {{city}} and across {{county}} decide when removal makes sense, and then we do it safely.

## When should a tree come down?

Not every sick-looking tree needs to go. Sometimes a good trim fixes the problem. But it's time to think about removal when you see:

- **Dead or mostly dead branches** in more than half of the crown.
- **A trunk that leans more than it used to**, or soil that's cracking or lifting on one side.
- **Deep cracks, splits or hollow spots** in the trunk or where big limbs meet.
- **Mushrooms or fungus** growing at the base, which can mean rot in the roots.
- **Oak wilt** in a red oak. Red oaks usually die fast once infected and can spread the disease. See our [oak wilt page](/oak-wilt/) for the signs.
- **Roots damaging** your foundation, driveway or sewer line.
- **A tree in the way** of a new addition, pool, fence or driveway.

If you're not sure, we'll walk the yard with you and give you an honest opinion. If a tree can be saved with [trimming](/tree-trimming/), we'll tell you.

## How tree removal works, step by step

1. **Free estimate.** We look at the tree, what's around it and how we can get equipment in. You get a clear written quote.
2. **Plan the job.** We figure out where each piece will go. In open yards we may drop the tree in one piece. Near homes, we take it down in sections.
3. **Protect your property.** We move what we can, lay down mats if needed and set up a safe work zone. Kids and pets stay inside.
4. **Cut and lower.** A climber or lift cuts limbs and trunk sections, often lowering them with ropes so they don't hit anything on the way down.
5. **Chip and haul.** Branches go through the chipper. Logs are hauled away or cut and left for you if you want firewood.
6. **Clean up.** We rake and blow off driveways, walks and patios. The stump is cut low, or ground out if you added [stump grinding](/stump-grinding/).

## What affects the cost of tree removal

We don't post one flat price, because every tree is different. These are the main things that change the price:

- **Size of the tree.** Taller trees with thick trunks take more time, labor and disposal.
- **Where it stands.** A tree in an open yard is simpler than one leaning over your roof, a pool or power lines.
- **Access.** If a truck or lift can't reach the tree, more of the work is done by hand and by climbing.
- **Health of the tree.** Dead or rotten wood can be brittle and less safe to climb, which can slow things down.
- **Extras.** Stump grinding, hauling the wood away or leaving cut firewood can change the total.
- **Emergency work.** A tree that's already on a house needs special care and is usually a bigger job.

## Safety around power lines

Never try to cut a tree that is touching or close to a power line. Call your electric company first. Utilities often handle trees touching their main lines. Once the line is safe, we can take care of the rest of the tree.
