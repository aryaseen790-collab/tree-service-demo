Lots of land in Central Texas fills in fast. Ashe juniper (most people call it cedar), mesquite, hackberry and thick brush can take over a lot or pasture in just a few years. That growth hides fence lines, crowds out good trees, eats up water and can raise wildfire risk in dry summers. Land clearing gets your property back under control so you can build, fence, graze or just enjoy it.

## When you might need land clearing

- **You're building** a home, shop, barn or driveway and need the site opened up.
- **Fence lines are overgrown** and you can't get to them for repairs.
- **Cedar and brush are taking over** a pasture or the area under your big trees.
- **You just bought land** and want to see what you actually have.
- **You want to reduce fire risk** by thinning brush near your home and outbuildings.
- **Storms left a mess** of fallen trees and limbs across your property.

## Selective clearing vs. full clearing

**Full clearing** removes nearly everything from an area, which is common for a building pad or driveway. **Selective clearing** removes brush, cedar and weak or unwanted trees while keeping the good ones. For most homeowners and small landowners, selective clearing gives the best result. Mature live oaks, pecans and cedar elms take decades to grow. Keeping them adds shade, value and beauty.

## How our land clearing process works

1. **Walk the property.** We walk the land with you to see what's there and learn your goals.
2. **Mark what stays.** We flag the trees and areas to protect, plus the boundaries of the work.
3. **Plan the work.** We choose the right equipment for your ground and the size of the job, and plan how debris will be handled.
4. **Clear.** Brush, cedar and unwanted trees are cut and removed, working carefully around the trees you're keeping.
5. **Handle debris.** Material is chipped, mulched or hauled away based on your plan.
6. **Stumps.** We can cut stumps low or grind them below ground with [stump grinding](/stump-grinding/), which matters for building pads and mowed areas.
7. **Final walk-through.** We walk the site with you to make sure it's what you wanted.

## Protecting your oaks during clearing

Oak wilt is common in Central Texas. Wounds on oak trees, even from equipment bumping the bark, can let the disease in, especially in spring. We work carefully around oaks and follow seasonal advice on cutting oaks. Learn more about [oak wilt care](/oak-wilt/).

## What affects the cost of land clearing

Every property is different, so we price clearing after a site visit. Main cost factors:

- **Size of the area** to be cleared.
- **How thick the growth is.** Light brush is quicker than dense cedar or big trees.
- **Size and number of trees** being removed.
- **Ground conditions** like slopes, rocks, creeks or wet soil.
- **Selective vs. full clearing.** Working around keeper trees takes more care and time.
- **Debris handling.** Mulching on site, chipping or hauling away all change the price.
- **Stump work.** Cutting stumps low vs. grinding them out.
