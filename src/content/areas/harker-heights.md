Harker Heights sits between Killeen and Belton, with hilly neighborhoods that roll down toward Stillhouse Hollow Lake. Homes here often sit on rocky limestone with thin soil, and many lots have live oaks and Ashe juniper (cedar) that were there long before the houses. It's a great place to live, and a place where tree work takes some planning.

## Tree work on hills and rocky ground

On flat lots, a crew can drive equipment right up to a tree. In Harker Heights, slopes, retaining walls and rock outcrops often get in the way. We plan each job around the ground:

- **Rigging and lowering** wood on slopes so it can't roll into fences, pools or homes.
- **Choosing the right equipment** for tight access between houses and down steep yards.
- **Stump grinding in rocky soil**, where we check for rock first so the quote is accurate. See our [stump grinding](/stump-grinding/) page for more.

## Cedar, brush and fire safety

Ashe juniper grows well on Harker Heights' rocky hillsides and can take over a backyard. It's also very flammable when dry. In hot, windy summers, thinning cedar and removing dead brush close to your home is a smart step. We can clear cedar and grind stumps, or do a larger selective clearing job that leaves your best oaks standing. Learn more on our [land clearing](/land-clearing/) page.

## Live oaks, red oaks and oak wilt

Harker Heights has plenty of live oaks and Texas red oaks, and both are at risk from oak wilt. Red oaks can die within weeks once infected, and live oaks can pass the disease through connected roots. To lower the risk:

- Avoid pruning oaks from February through June.
- Paint fresh oak wounds right away.
- Watch for yellow-veined leaves or fast leaf drop, and call early.

Our [oak wilt care](/oak-wilt/) page has the details.

## Wind and storms on the hills

Homes on hilltops and open slopes can feel the full force of spring storms and gusty north winds. Shallow roots in thin, rocky soil make some trees more likely to tip over in a strong storm. Thinning dense crowns and removing weak limbs with [tree trimming](/tree-trimming/) helps trees handle the wind.
